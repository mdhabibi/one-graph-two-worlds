**MIT** for original example/build/checking code. These terms have been prepared,
not activated as a public-release declaration.

## Book text and original figures

its conditions: credit, a license link, and an indication of changes. This choice
supports translations, teaching, and adaptations. The granted freedoms cannot
simply be withdrawn from compliant recipients after publication.

Proposed CC BY scope: book prose, original diagrams, and original synthetic
catalog data. Proposed MIT scope: lab scripts, query files, build/check/export
