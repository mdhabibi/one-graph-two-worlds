# Knowledge Graphs — version 0.2.0

A public review edition of *Knowledge Graphs: Semantic Graphs and Property Graphs*
by Dr. Mahdi Habibi.

This edition adds a conditional recommendation-query equivalence argument and
an inspectable bounded experiment. Five integrated extensions cover semantic
evolution and dated policy replay, eligibility versus suitability, reasoning
value versus cost, entity identity and source evidence, and missing-data validation.
The contents, explained review questions, glossary, browser chapters, exercises
and companion navigation are synchronized.
